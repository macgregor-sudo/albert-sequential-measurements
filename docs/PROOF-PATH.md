# Proof path of the compared theorems

`src/Solution.lean` repeats the twelve statements of `src/Challenge.lean` over `src/ChallengeDefs.lean`, a verbatim
copy of the Challenge's definitions, and proves each by one appeal to a bridge theorem in namespace
`AlbertChallenge.Bridge`. The bridges derive the statements from theorems of the development
(namespace `AlbertSequential`, except `LemmaB.*`, which is top level). Every bridge declaration is
in `AlbertChallenge.Bridge` and is named without that prefix; module names are given in parentheses
at first use.

## The bridges

On the Albert side the Challenge's definitions and the development's (`Defs`, `Interfaces.Geometry`,
`Interfaces.WordsAndCertificates`, `ThirteenTracesAndAform`) have identical bodies. A plain `rfl`
between nested products does not finish in practice, because the definitional checker evaluates
octonion products coordinatewise, so `Bridge.Albert` goes through the identity map
`toT : AlbertChallenge.Albert → AlbertSequential.Albert` and type-homogeneous rewrite lemmas:
`jordanMul_toT`, `quadraticMap_toT`, `isAtom_toT`, `isDensity_toT`, `wordProbability_toT`,
`generates_toT`, `fourPointInvariant_toT`, `quadrupleM_eq` (`quadrupleM = maximizerConfig`) and
`quadrupleS_eq` (`quadrupleS = fixedS`).

On the model side the development states its exclusions for `AlgebraicProjectiveData`: a real
`*`-algebra, self-adjoint idempotents and a positive Hermitian normalised functional.
`Bridge.Models` converts each Challenge class into it.

- `wordOperator_palindrome`: for self-adjoint idempotent tests, `Q_1 ⋯ Q_m ⋯ Q_1 = a* a`, where `a` is
  the product of the outcome operators in reverse order.
- `ofAlgebraicModel`: an `AlgebraicModel` with the same word values.
- `ofProjectiveRepresentation`: a real or complex representation on `H` gives an algebraic model on the
  bounded operators regarded as a real algebra (`HilbertOperatorAlgebra`), with functional
  `T ↦ Re φ(T)` restricted to self-adjoint operators and extended by symmetrisation. For `𝕜 = ℝ` a
  state in the Challenge's sense need not be Hermitian, so the symmetrisation is needed.
- `ofQuaternionicRepresentation`: a quaternionic representation gives an algebraic model on the
  commutant `S.linearOperators` itself. The `ℍ`-valued inner product of `RightQuaternionicStructure`
  is not used.

`Bridge.Exclusion` turns the development's finite word functionals (lists of weighted words) into
`Finset` sums (`coefficient`, `sum_coefficient`, `evaluate_eq_sum`). `Bridge.C12` goes the other way for
complex models: from the development's `HilbertProjectiveData` over `ℂ` it builds a Challenge state
`ψ(T) = g(T) − i·g(iT)`, where `g` is the state applied to the self-adjoint part, proves it `ℂ`-linear
and positive (`complexState`, `complexState_selfAdjoint`, `toChallenge`, `toChallenge_probability`).
`Bridge.Matrix` turns a density matrix `σ` and Hermitian idempotent matrices on `ℂ^d` into a Challenge
representation on `EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin d)` with state `T ↦ tr(σ · [T])` (`matrixRepresentation`,
`matrixRepresentation_probability`); positivity is `tr(σX) = tr(√σ X √σ) ≥ 0` (`trace_mul_nonneg`). `Bridge.Criterion` adds that this state
is `T ↦ tr(σ T)` for the positive operator of `σ` (`matrixRepresentation_state`, `trace_matToCLM`,
`matToCLM_isPositive`), which is `HasDensityState`.

## 1. `no_representation_of_generates`

Bridge: `no_representation_bridge`, `not_matches_of_algebraic` (`Bridge.Exclusion`).

1. `Bridge.Models` turns a matching representation or algebraic model into a matching
   `AlgebraicProjectiveData`; `generates_toT`, `isAtom_toT` and `wordProbability_toT` restate the Albert
   side.
2. `exists_generatedCyclicJordanMap` (`GeneratedCyclicReconstruction`): a matching model gives a Jordan
   homomorphism from the subalgebra generated by the atoms into the endomorphisms of the model's cyclic
   word space, sending `e` to the identity. With `nativeOrbitSpan_eq_top_of_generates`
   (`NativeReflectionOrbit`) the generated subalgebra is `J`: `exists_albertCyclicJordanMap`
   (`AlbertCyclicReconstruction`).
3. `albertJordanHom_eq_zero` (`AlbertExceptionality`): every real-linear Jordan homomorphism from `J`
   into an associative, possibly nonunital, real algebra is zero. Its proof evaluates the tetrahedron
   identity (`TetrahedronEngine`) at the configuration `S`, whose tetrahedron statistic `𝒟` is nonzero.
4. `algebraicProjective_not_matches_generating_atoms` (`AlgebraicProjectiveNoGo`): by 2 and 3 the
   identity of the cyclic word space would be zero, which `cyclic_endomorphism_unit_ne_zero`
   (`CyclicWordQuotient`) excludes.

## 2. `linear_separator_of_generates`

Bridge: `linear_separator_bridge`, `nonneg_of_algebraic` (`Bridge.Exclusion`).

1. `noMomentMatch_generating_atoms` (`AlbertUniformSeparation`): by theorem 1 at the algebraic level,
   no admissible moment functional (a normalised, Hermitian, positive functional on the group algebra
   `ℝ[ℤ₂^{*n}]`, the moment data of an algebraic model) matches the Albert probabilities from any state.
2. `uniformMomentSeparator_generating_atoms`: by compactness of the set of admissible moment
   functionals (`AlgebraicSeparationAssembly`, `CompactMomentSeparation`), one finite word functional and
   one `γ > 0` work for all states: at most `−γ` on the Albert tables from every state.
3. `wordFunctional_nonnegative_on_algebraic_models` (`AlgebraicSeparationAssembly`): the functional is
   nonnegative on every algebraic model.
4. `Bridge.Models` applies 3 to real, complex and quaternionic representations, and
   `evaluate_eq_sum` writes the functional as a `Finset` sum with merged duplicate words.

## 3. `seven_steps_exclude`

Bridge: `seven_steps_bridge`, `seven_of_algebraic`, `six_of_algebraic` (`Bridge.Seven`).

1. `fourPointInvariant_pos_iff` (`ThreeAtomAndFourPoint`) turns `Generates p` into `0 < 𝒜`.
2. `LengthSeven.tableValues_native` and `lengthSeven_e3_rules` (`LengthSeven.TableValues`): the Albert
   word probabilities satisfy the short-history relations, and a model that matches the yes-first words
   of length at most seven from `e/3` satisfies them too.
3. `LengthSeven.LemmaA.lemmaA` (`LengthSeven.LemmaA.Main`): those relations fix all 406 Gram entries
   between 28 path vectors as polynomials in the thirteen traces.
4. `LengthSeven.TableModel.tableModel` (`TableModel.Main`): these entries equal, entry by entry, those of
   an explicit comparison form; this needs only `o_AB o_AC o_AD ≠ 0`.
5. `LemmaB.no_psd_realisation_traces` (`LemmaB.Conclusion`): when `𝒜 > 0` and `0 < o_Ax < 1`, three
   explicit combinations of the 28 vectors have a Gram matrix of negative determinant, so no positive
   semidefinite form has these entries. On the Albert side the hypotheses come from the trace relation
   (`albert_fourAtom_trace_relation`; `LengthSeven.Uniform.native_traceRelation`,
   `LengthSeven.Uniform.native_Acal`, module `Uniform.Main`).
6. `LengthSeven.TableModel.no_walkForm_of_gram28Rules` and `LengthSeven.Uniform.uniform_walkForm`
   combine 2 to 5; `LengthSeven.Uniform.uniform_lengthSeven_e3_algebraic` (from `e/3`) and
   `LengthSeven.Uniform.uniform_lengthSix_pA_algebraic` (from `p_A`, through
   `LengthSeven.Uniform.probability_cons_yesA_of_certain`) state the exclusion for algebraic models
   (`Uniform.Main`).
7. `Bridge.Models` carries it to the Challenge's classes.

The walk reduction of core `lem:cer:reduce` is not used, and the comparison form is used directly,
without the Clifford-algebra normalisation of the printed proof.

## 4. `six_step_model`

Bridge: `six_step_model_bridge` (`Bridge.C12`).

1. `fourPointInvariant_pos_of_generates` (`Bridge.AlbertHeadlines`) gives `0 < 𝒜`.
2. `MatchingModels.prop_cer_quatpair_c` (`MatchingModels.QuatPair`): two quadruples of atoms in a
   quaternionic subalgebra whose mixture with weights `λ`, `1 − λ` has the thirteen traces of `p`.
3. `MatchingModels.nativeWordProbability_e3_affine_le_six` (`MatchingModels.UniversalSixAffinity`): from
   `e/3`, every word probability of length at most six is affine in the double-pair traces, so the
   mixture matches the Albert table on those words.
4. `MatchingModels.c12BlockMixtureModel` and `MatchingModels.quatProj6_wordProb_e3`
   (`MatchingModels.MatrixClassIBridge`) realise the
   mixture on `ℂ¹²` with block projections of rank two; `MatchingModels.prop_cer_universalsix_c12`
   (`MatchingModels.UniversalSix`) states it as a `HilbertProjectiveData` on `EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin 12)`.
5. `toChallenge` (`Bridge.C12`) converts it to a Challenge representation with the same word values.

## 5. `least_separating_length`

Bridge: `least_separating_bridge` (`Bridge.Least`). It uses theorems 3 and 4 only;
`MatchingModels.LMinSeven` is not used.

1. `7` is in the set: theorem 3 at universe `0`, since every yes-first word of length at most seven is a
   word of length at most seven (`yesFirst_subset`).
2. No `L ≤ 6` is in the set: the `ℂ¹²` model of theorem 4 matches all words of length at most six,
   hence of length at most `L` (`wordsUpTo_mono`), and `EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin 12)` lies in `Type`.

## 6. `fourPoint_criterion`

Bridge: `fourPoint_criterion_bridge` (`Bridge.AlbertHeadlines`).

1. `lemma_A_normal_general` (`ThreeAtomAndFourPoint`): an automorphism of `J` moves any four atoms to a
   normal form.
2. `fourPointInvariant_nonneg`: `𝒜` is invariant under automorphisms
   (`fourPointInvariant_automorphism_invariant`, `ThirteenTracesAndAform`) and nonnegative in the normal
   form.
3. `fourPointInvariant_pos_iff`: `𝒜 > 0` exactly when the atoms generate `J`.
4. `fourPointInvariant_toT` and `generates_toT` restate both parts.

## 8. `quadrupleS_spec`

## 9. `three_atoms`

Bridge: `three_atoms_bridge`, `three_atoms_matching` (`Bridge.Matrix`).

1. `complexTripleContract_holds` (`ThreeAtomAndFourPoint`), through its field `rank_one_statistics`:
   for three atoms and a state, rank-one projections `P_i` and a density matrix `σ` on `ℂ³` with
   `Re tr(σ · Q_1 ⋯ Q_m ⋯ Q_1)` equal to every Albert word probability. Its proof moves the three atoms
   into a copy of `H₃(ℂ)` by an automorphism (`lemma_A_three`).
2. `matrixRepresentation` turns these into a Challenge representation on `ℂ³`.
3. Non-generation: if the atoms generated `J`, theorem 1 at universe `0` and `ρ = p 0` would exclude the
   model of step 2.

## 10. `zero_invariant_model`

Bridge: `zero_invariant_bridge` (`Bridge.Matrix`).

1. `fourPoint_zero_c6_representation` (`ThreeAtomAndFourPoint`): if `𝒜 = 0`, the atoms lie in a copy of
   `H₃(ℍ)` (`fourPointInvariant_eq_zero_iff`), and there are Hermitian idempotent `6 × 6` complex
   matrices of trace two such that for every state a density matrix `σ` reproduces every word.
2. `matrixRepresentation` turns them into a Challenge representation on `ℂ⁶`.

## 11. `albert_criterion`

Bridge: `albert_criterion_bridge` (`Bridge.Criterion`).

1. `exists_proper_native_of_not_generates`: atoms that do not generate `J` lie in a proper unital
   subalgebra `B` of the development's `J`.
2. `lem_five_sub` (`SubalgebraCoordsAndLine`): a proper unital subalgebra lies in a line subalgebra or
   in a quaternionic subalgebra. These carry unital Jordan embeddings into complex Hermitian matrices,
   `lineCliffordEmbed` (`24 × 24`, trace form scaled by `8`) and `symplecticQuatEmbed` (`6 × 6`, scaled
   by `2`).
3. `matrix_data_of_proper`: the images `P_i` of the atoms are Hermitian idempotents that do not depend
   on the state, and for each state `ρ`, `suppliedEmbedding_scaled_state_transport`
   (`SuppliedEmbeddingStateTransport`) gives a density matrix `σ` with
   `Re tr(σ · Q_1 ⋯ Q_m ⋯ Q_1) = Pr_ρ(w)` for every word. `matrixRepresentation` turns `(P, σ)` into a
   Challenge representation on `ℂ^d` with state `T ↦ tr(σ T)`; this is (b), with `M₀` built from the
   state `diag(1, 0, 0)` (`exists_isDensity`).
4. (b) ⇒ (c): apply (b) at `diag(1, 0, 0)`; the representation lies in `Type`.
5. (c) ⇒ not generating: `no_representation_bridge` at universe `0` (theorem 1).
6. (a): not generating gives the proper `B` of step 1, and `proper_iff_special` (`SubalgebraCoordsAndLine`)
   gives a unital injective Jordan embedding of `B` into complex Hermitian `d × d` matrices,
   `d > 0`; conversely such an embedding of a subalgebra that contains the atoms makes it proper by the
   same equivalence, so the atoms do not generate `J`.

The route is that of `cor_helly_operational_if` without its Helly step, which is not needed when the
whole family lies in one proper subalgebra.

## 12. `isAtom_iff_primitive`

Bridge: `isAtom_iff_primitive_bridge` (`Bridge.Atoms`), from `NativeAnalysis.isAtom_iff_primitive`.

1. `NativeAnalysis.native_idempotent_trace_cases` (`NativeIdempotentClassification`): an idempotent of `J` has trace
   `0`, `1`, `2` or `3`; trace `0` only for `0` (`NativeAnalysis.isPositive_trace_eq_zero`, `NativeAnalysis.isPositive_idempotent`).
2. `NativeAnalysis.one_le_trace_of_idempotent`: a nonzero idempotent has trace at least one, so a sum of two nonzero
   orthogonal idempotents has trace at least two and is not an atom.
3. `NativeAnalysis.exists_orthogonal_split`: an idempotent of trace two or three is moved by an automorphism of `J`
   (`NativeAnalysis.nativeDiagonalization`) to a diagonal `0/1` matrix with at least two entries `1`; splitting off one
   diagonal unit and moving back gives two nonzero idempotents `q`, `r` with `q ∘ r = 0`.

## Exact strength of the named steps

`no_representation_of_generates`: for any finite family of atoms that generates `J` and any element `ρ`
of `J`, no real or complex representation (scalar field any `RCLike` field in `Type`), no realised
quaternionic representation and no algebraic model, with carrier in universe `Type v`, agrees with
`wordProbability p ρ` on every word. Step 4 holds for any initial element; positivity is required only
of the model's functional.

`linear_separator_of_generates`: existence of `L`, a finite word set, coefficients and `γ > 0`, with no
bound on any of them. The compared statement covers real, complex and quaternionic representations;
step 3 covers algebraic models too, but that is not compared. The Albert side is bounded for every
state (`IsDensity`), not for arbitrary `ρ`.

`seven_steps_exclude`: for every generating quadruple, matching is excluded on the words of length at
most seven that begin with "yes" on test `0` (from `e/3`) and on all words of length at most six from
`p 0`. No margin is stated; the existence of one (core `thm:cer:uniform`(b)) is `Ends.uniform_b`, not
compared, and the "every state" version (core `cor:cer:everystate`) is `BlockB.cor_cer_everystate`, not
compared; both are proved in the repository.

`six_step_model`: existence of some representation on `ℂ¹²` matching the words of length at most six
from `e/3`. Steps 2 to 4 construct a specific one, a mixture of two quaternionic quadruples with block
projections of rank two (`MatchingModels.prop_cer_universalsix_witness`), but the compared statement does not record
its form or the consequences that no linear equality or inequality on words of length at most six
separates the table (`MatchingModels.no_classI_linearEq_le_six`,
`MatchingModels.no_classI_linearIneq_le_six`).

`least_separating_length`: Hilbert spaces in `Type` only; real, complex and quaternionic
representations, not algebraic models. Its upper half also follows, in every universe, from
`seven_steps_exclude`.

`fourPoint_criterion`: parts (i) and (iii) of core `thm:fourpoint`. Parts (ii) and (iv) are
`thm_fourpoint`, not compared.

`quadrupleM_spec`: the bound for all atoms and attainment at `M`. Uniqueness of the maximiser up to
automorphism is in `prop_max`, not compared.

`quadrupleS_spec`: the atom property and `𝒜 = 1/5832`. That `S` generates `J` follows with
`fourPoint_criterion`.

`three_atoms`: non-generation, and a representation on `ℂ³` for each state. Step 1 gives rank-one
projections and the containing copy of `H₃(ℂ)`; the compared statement records neither, and lets the
representation depend on the state.

`zero_invariant_model`: a representation on `ℂ⁶` for each state when `𝒜 = 0`. Step 1 gives more: the
atoms lie in a copy of `H₃(ℍ)`, and the projections are fixed and of rank two, with only `σ` depending
on the state. The compared statement records none of this. With `fourPoint_criterion`, `𝒜 = 0` is
exactly non-generation, so this covers every quadruple that does not generate `J`. For every finite
family, `albert_criterion`(b) gives fixed projections on `ℂ⁶` or `ℂ²⁴`.

`albert_criterion`: three equivalences, each with `¬ Generates p` on the left, for any `n`. In (b)
the representation `M₀` serves only to fix the projections; its own state plays no role. In (c) the
witness may be a representation of any of the three kinds or an algebraic model, with carrier in
`Type`; the converse direction in every universe is theorem 1. Step 3 constructs the representations
of (b) explicitly, but the statement records only `d ∈ {6, 24}`, the common projections and the density
state.

`isAtom_iff_primitive`: an equivalence for every element of `J`, with no hypothesis; orthogonality of
idempotents is `q ∘ r = 0`.
