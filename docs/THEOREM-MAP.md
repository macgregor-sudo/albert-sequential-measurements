# Theorem map

Results proved in the repository, with their plain meaning, the paper statement they belong to, the
Lean declarations and the module. The [status ledger](../README.md#status-of-every-numbered-statement)
names, for every numbered statement, the declarations that prove the printed statement; this map adds
the unnumbered results, the supporting results and the compared theorems, and for many numbered
statements it names declarations with a narrower or a different statement. Where a row says
"narrower" or names what a declaration does not contain, that describes the declaration in the row,
not the status of the paper statement, which the ledger gives.

Declarations are named without the prefix `AlbertSequential`, except those beginning with
`AlbertChallenge` (the compared theorems and the bridge layer) and `LemmaB` or `Glennie`, which are
top-level namespaces. Within a cell, a name without a dot after a dotted one has the same prefix.
"Yes" in the last column means the result is one of the twelve theorems compared with
`Challenge.lean`; "no" means it is proved and kernel-checked but not compared.
[STATEMENTS.md](STATEMENTS.md) gives the definitions behind the compared theorems, and
[MODULES.md](MODULES.md) locates the modules.
